Awareness helps users understand why UV protection matters. This page highlights UV exposure risks, trends, and simple facts that support safer outdoor choices.

Awareness Note

UV damage is not always immediately visible. Even on cooler or cloudy days, high UV levels can still affect the skin and eyes. Checking UV before going outside helps users make safer daily decisions.

Why UV Awareness Matters

UV 8+
At higher UV levels, skin can be damaged more quickly without sun protection. This is why users should check UV, wear suitable clothing, and apply sunscreen before going outside.

Peak UV Time

10AM–4PM
UV is often strongest in the middle part of the day. During this period, users should reduce direct sun exposure and combine protection strategies.

Key Awareness Messages

UV can still be high on mild days Temperature does not always reflect UV intensity, so users should not rely only on how hot the weather feels.
Protection works best in combination Sunscreen, protective clothing, sunglasses, hats, and shade are more effective when used together.
Checking UV supports better decisions Knowing the UV level helps users choose suitable sunscreen, clothing, and outdoor timing.
